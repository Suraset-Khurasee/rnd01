Project brief

CE-0169: Nero Robotic Arm Teleoperation — evaluating control methods for public demonstrations.

Project at a glance

  • Organization: Science Centre Singapore
  • Mentor: Mr Jonathan
  • Project start: 11 November 2026
  • Participants: 3
  • Equipment: AgileX Nero robotic arm

What you will do

Students will safely operate a robotic arm through teleoperation, research the advantages and limitations of different control methods, and test simple pick-and-place activities.

  • Assemble, connect, upgrade, and calibrate the robotic arm
  • Configure teleoperation software and learn robot coordinates
  • Develop Python programs for basic manipulation tasks
  • Integrate camera-based computer vision for object detection
  • Document setup, trials, findings, and research

Minimum goal

Successfully control the robotic arm to pick up and place objects in designated locations.

Stretch goal: Complete the task with smooth, accurate movements and design a showcase for the Science Centre.

Prerequisites

Programming experience and comfort with Linux are preferred. Previous experience with AI and robotic arms is helpful but not required.
