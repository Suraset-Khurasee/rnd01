# Project brief

**Project code:** CE-0169  
**Title:** Nero Robotic Arm Teleoperation: Evaluating Control Methods for Public Demonstrations

## Overview

Robots are increasingly used in manufacturing, healthcare, and logistics. In this project, students learn to safely operate a robotic arm through teleoperation and research the advantages and limitations of different control methods for simple pick-and-place activities.

## Responsibilities

- Assemble, connect, upgrade, and calibrate the robotic arm.
- Configure teleoperation software.
- Learn the robot coordinate system and movement controls.
- Develop Python programs for basic manipulation tasks.
- Integrate camera-based computer vision for object detection.
- Test pick-and-place behavior.
- Document research, setup, trials, and findings.

## Skills

1. Assemble and connect a robotic arm.
2. Configure teleoperation software.
3. Understand coordinates and robot movement.
4. Operate the robot with a controller.
5. Test a pick-and-place system.
6. Research and document results.

## Prerequisites

Programming experience and comfort with Linux are preferred. Experience with AI and robotic arms is helpful.

The final product and hardware remain on site unless CRADL staff explicitly state otherwise.
