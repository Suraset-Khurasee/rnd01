Milestones

A nine-day path from safety and setup to a documented public demonstration.

Days 1–2 · Foundations

Complete administration, safety briefings, skills training, and workshops as needed. Introduce the robot, equipment, and safe operating procedures. Record the setup and trial-run workflow.

Days 3–4 · Control and research

Learn robot configurations, coordinate systems, and controls. Complete the trainer’s safe-use assessment, research control methods, and develop ideas for a suitable showcase.

Learn→Test→Showcase

Days 5–8 · Testing

Research and test ways to conduct the showcase. Compare control approaches, refine pick-and-place behavior, and document every setup, result, and change.

Day 9 · Share

Complete housekeeping, present the outcomes, and attend the certificate presentation.
