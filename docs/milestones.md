# Milestones

## Day 1

- Administration and safety briefing.
- Skills training and laboratory equipment workshops as needed.
- Mechanical workbench, CAD, laser cutting, and literature review workshops as needed.

## Day 2

- Introduction to robotics, equipment, and safety.
- Learn how to document workflow and trial-run results.
- Document how to set up and safely use the robotic arm.

## Day 3

- Learn robot configurations and controls.
- Document the configuration and controls.

## Day 4

- Complete the trainer assessment on safe use.
- Research ways to control the robotic arm.
- Develop ideas for a suitable robotic-arm showcase.

## Days 5–8

- Research and test showcase approaches.
- Document the setup, control method, results, and changes for each trial.

## Day 9

- Housekeeping.
- Presentation.
- Certificate presentation.
