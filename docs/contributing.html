Working safely

Safe operation and disciplined documentation are part of the project, not separate from it.

Operating workflow

  1. Complete the safety briefing and trainer assessment before independent operation.
  2. Check connections, calibration, workspace, and emergency procedures before each trial.
  3. Change one control variable at a time and record the result.
  4. Leave the workspace and robot in the documented safe state after testing.

Quality bar

For every experiment, capture the setup, control method, object and destination, observed movement, accuracy, issues, and next change. Use evidence from trials when comparing control methods.

Important: The final product remains on site unless CRADL staff explicitly state otherwise. Do not take the robotic arm or project hardware home.
